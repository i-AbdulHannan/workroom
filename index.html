02 · the founder

Workroom did not start as a product idea. It started as a frustration that kept repeating on real client projects.

Abdul Hannan Founder · AI-Integrated Product Engineer Pakistan

The problem he kept seeing

Working with clients across WhatsApp, email and scattered tools meant the same things broke every time. Files disappeared. Approvals were forgotten. Payments needed chasing. Clients kept asking the same questions because there was never one place to look.

Why he built Workroom

After watching too many projects stall over missed messages and lost versions, Abdul Hannan decided to build the room he always wished existed. Not another dashboard. One calm, professional space where a project can actually live, and where the important parts never get buried.

What makes it different

Workroom is built specifically for agencies and freelancers who work directly with clients. Progress, files, approvals, payments, conversations and delivery all live together in the same room. No forced cloud lock-in. No expensive dependencies. Just a clear, practical place to run client work.
